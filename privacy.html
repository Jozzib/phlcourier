Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

PHL Express collects personal data necessary for secure package delivery and freight handling, including sender and recipient names, shipping addresses, telephone numbers, and tracking reference details.

2. How We Handle Data

All data submitted through our portal is encrypted in transit. We utilize tracking data solely for the purpose of dispatching, sorting, and delivering parcels to their designated destinations. We do not sell or lease customer data to third-party advertisers.

3. Contact Regarding Data Security

If you have questions regarding data handling or privacy compliance, contact our compliance office at support@phlexpress.com.
